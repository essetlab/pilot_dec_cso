# CSO Learning Hub v1.1 Decision Log

## 1. What changed from the original consolidated outline

The original consolidated outline remains a broad master outline with many possible product areas. Version 1.1 narrows that material into an implementation-ready pilot scope.

Key changes:
- Reframed the current product as a learner-centered e-learning pilot with future-ready architecture.
- Separated current pilot scope from future-stage specifications.
- Added explicit non-extractive data and privacy rules.
- Added approved public positioning: "Learn. Connect. Grow."
- Added the approved public promise: “A practical learning hub where local and grassroots CSOs build skills, share experience, and grow their capacity to lead change.”
- Added specific HRBA course overview correction guidance.
- Added protected HRBA launchToken integration rules.
- Added certificate disclaimer and verification safety rules.
- Added a build-now backlog and staged implementation roadmap.
- Marked community, practical proof, verifier, organization profile, offline sync, authoring expansion, and intelligence features as future-stage unless separately approved.

## 2. What is now confirmed as pilot scope

Confirmed pilot scope:
- Learner registration.
- Learner sign-in.
- Course catalog.
- Course overview pages.
- HRBA course access through the Hub.
- Protected launchToken-based external course launch.
- Progress tracking.
- Final assessment callback.
- 80% certificate eligibility rule.
- Downloadable PDF certificate.
- Public certificate verification.
- Learner dashboard.
- My Courses.
- Certificates page.
- Profile/settings.
- Course feedback form.
- Aggregate-only pilot monitoring.
- Essential Help / Privacy / Terms / Accessibility pages.

Pilot principle: the Hub must remain learner-centered and must not become a donor portal, course-authoring platform, or surveillance dashboard.

## 3. What is moved to later-stage specification

Moved to later-stage specification:
- External Verifier / Partner Verifier role.
- Consent-based organization profile.
- Optional practical proof / verified achievement pathway.
- Peer learning and LALINKage community.
- Community resource validation workflow.
- Learner directory.
- Forums and interaction spaces.
- Offline sync and conflict-resolution architecture.
- Course/assessment versioning expansion.
- Capability intelligence layer.
- Course creator / reviewer / publisher workflow expansion beyond pilot needs.
- AI-assisted authoring governance.
- Multilingual/local-language expansion.
- Advanced partner-safe reporting.

These may be described in public copy only as future-aware vision. They must not appear as active pilot modules unless implemented and accepted in a future stage.

## 4. What is explicitly out of scope

Explicitly out of current pilot scope:
- Full donor portal.
- Public organization scoring.
- Blockchain or immutable ledger claims.
- Algorithmic CSO reputation ranking.
- Full offline sync engine.
- Open public resource uploads.
- Full forum/community platform.
- Full public or multi-team course authoring tool.
- Reviewer/publisher dashboards.
- Advanced analytics/capability intelligence dashboards.
- Public practical proof gallery.
- Public exposure of CSO weaknesses.
- Donor access to raw learner records.

## 5. Key privacy/non-extractive architecture decisions

Decisions:
- Public and learner-facing URLs must not expose raw internal IDs.
- Public certificate verification must not expose learner email, assessment answers, private progress, portfolio content, private organization details, or internal IDs.
- Feedback is for course improvement, not individual learner judgment.
- Pilot monitoring is aggregate-only.
- Raw feedback text, individual weaknesses, assessment answers, portfolio outputs, organization weaknesses, and raw learner records must not be exposed to donors.
- Practical outputs are private by default.
- Optional proof is separate from certificate completion and must be future-stage, consent-based, and safety-reviewed.
- The HRBA app must not generate certificates; the Hub remains responsible for progress, final assessment result, certificate eligibility, certificate generation, and verification.
- Safe-use reminders must appear wherever learners type or submit content.

Required safe-use wording: "Do not include names, survivor stories, exact locations, complaints, political details, safeguarding cases, or confidential organizational information."

Required certificate disclaimer: "This certificate confirms that the named learner completed the course requirements and passed the final assessment. It does not replace organizational due diligence, safeguarding review, legal compliance checks, or partnership assessment."
